Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Updated October 24, 2026 · SickleStrong LLC

The short version

  • You decide what goes into SickleStrong, and who can see each child's information.
  • We never sell your information, and we never use it for advertising.
  • Only people you personally invite to a specific child can see that child's records.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

SickleStrong is a care-tracking app for families managing sickle cell disease, operated by SickleStrong LLC in Alabama, United States ("we," "us"). This policy covers the SickleStrong app at sicklestrong-v2.netlify.app and this website.

Questions or requests: hello@sicklestrong.app.

2. Who can use SickleStrong

Accounts are for adults 18 and older (parents, guardians, caregivers, and adults tracking their own care) and for teens 13 to 17 whose parent or guardian lets them in.

  • Teens 13 to 17 can join when a parent or guardian invites them, or by signing up and naming a parent or guardian, whose email we keep with the request so they can approve it. The account opens nothing until that parent approves it. The parent chooses what the teen can see and do, can change or remove that access at any time, and can delete the teen's records.
  • Children under 13 cannot have an account. Their parent or guardian enters their information.

We do not knowingly allow anyone under 13 to create an account, or anyone 13 to 17 to use one without a parent's invite or approval. If you believe that has happened, email us and we will delete it.

Where it is stored

SickleStrong saves your entries on your own device first, so it works offline. If you create an account, your entries are also saved to our secure cloud database so you can sign in on another device and share with people you invite. If you use the app without an account, your information stays on that device only and we do not receive it.

Collected automatically

We use Simple Analytics, a privacy-focused service that uses no cookies and does not track individuals. It counts page visits and app actions (for example, "setup completed," "temperature logged," and whether a reading was a fever). These counts are not linked to your name, email, or account. Like any website, our hosting provider receives standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you connect.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the app: save, sync, and display your family's records.
  • To share records with the people you invite, at the access level you choose.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password resets.
  • To contact founding beta families about the program, if you signed up for it.
  • To improve SickleStrong, using the anonymous counts described above.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, use your health information for marketing, or share it with data brokers.

5. Who can see your information

People you invite

Each child has their own private circle. A person you invite can see only the child they were invited to, at the level you chose: view only, add pain and fever entries, or full access. Someone invited to one child cannot see a sibling's records. Parents on a child's circle can remove people at any time.

6. How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database access is checked for every request, per child, so one family cannot reach another's records.
  • Sign-in rejects passwords known to have appeared in data breaches.
  • Our database is backed up daily.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is active.

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete my account and enter your password. Your account is deleted right away, along with your access to every child. Copies in our daily backups are removed as those backups expire, within 7 days. If you can't sign in, email hello@sicklestrong.app from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Delete a child's records: a parent on that child's circle can permanently delete the child, and every record about them, inside the app.
  • Clear this device: Settings → Clear All Data removes everything stored on the phone or browser you're using.

If you share a child with other parents, deleting your account removes your access but does not delete that child for the other parents on their circle. Entries you added for that child stay in the child's history without your name. If you are a child's only parent, the app will ask you to invite another parent or delete that child's records first, so a child is never left with no one able to manage their care.

8. Your choices

  • See and download your data: Settings → Export All Data.
  • Correct it: edit any entry in the app, or email us.
  • Stop beta emails: reply to any email asking us to stop.
  • Turn off reminders: medication reminders use your device's notification setting, which you can turn off at any time.

9. Important to know

SickleStrong helps you organize information. It is not medical advice and does not replace your child's doctor. In an emergency, call 911.

SickleStrong is a tool your family uses on its own behalf. We are not a healthcare provider, so health-privacy laws that apply to doctors and hospitals, such as HIPAA, generally do not apply to us. We still treat your information with the same care.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change, we will update the date at the top and tell account holders by email or in the app before it takes effect.
